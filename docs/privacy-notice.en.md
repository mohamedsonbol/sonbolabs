# Privacy Notice — English approval draft

**Status:** Do not publish. This is the English approval draft used by the local `/privacy` implementation. Publication remains blocked pending the operational/legal checks in `PRIVACY-VERIFICATION.md`.

**Effective date:** 22 October 2026

## 1. Controller and contact details

Mohamed Sonbol Business Consulting, trading as Sonbol Labs, is the controller. It is established in Poland. For privacy questions or requests, email [ezzat@exceptionalgrp.com](mailto:ezzat@exceptionalgrp.com).

## 2. Scope and data collected

This notice covers site use, project enquiries and correspondence about prospective work. The contact form collects name, work email, company, optional website URL, project type, budget, timeline, message and selected language. Website and infrastructure providers may process technical and security data, such as IP address, request data and security events. The consent cookie, `sonbol_analytics_consent`, records only `granted` or `denied` for 180 days.

## 3. Purposes and lawful bases

We use enquiry data to assess, answer and follow up on a request, and to take steps at the visitor's request before a contract; where applicable this is necessary for pre-contractual steps and contract performance. We use limited technical and security data for security, abuse prevention, fault investigation and reliable operation under legitimate interests. We retain data where necessary for legal, tax, accounting or claims obligations under legal obligation and, where applicable, legitimate interests in legal claims. Optional analytics requires consent where consent is required; it can be refused or withdrawn through Privacy choices.

## 4. Contact enquiries and email

The endpoint validates submissions, checks origin and body size, and uses a honeypot. A successful enquiry is sent by Nodemailer through configured SMTP to the Hostinger business inquiry mailbox. The application does not store enquiries in an application database or CRM. Personal-Gmail forwarding has been removed, and new enquiries are not copied to that account.

## 5. Hosting, recipients and external services

The Next.js site is hosted on Hostinger and Cloudflare is in the traffic path. Hostinger processes contact enquiries in the business inquiry mailbox. Authorised people handling enquiries or delivering services may access relevant information. We do not sell personal data or use enquiries for a marketing mailing list. Third-party links are governed by their own notices.

## 6. Cookies and analytics

Essential technologies support site operation and security. GTM is loaded only if configured and the visitor grants analytics consent. Consent-gated, site-created events may contain structured interaction data such as CTA, selected project options, booking-link click or selected language. They are designed to exclude direct identifiers and free-text form values, but analytics data may still be personal data depending on the implementation and context. Actual products, tags, settings and retention must be verified before activation.

## 7. Retention

- Unconverted enquiries: no longer than 12 months after the last substantive interaction.
- Client records: for the period required by applicable contractual, tax, accounting and legal-claims requirements.
- Technical logs under our control: intended maximum of 90 days.
- Consent cookie: 180 days, unless changed or cleared earlier.

## 8. International transfers

Infrastructure, email, backup and analytics providers may process data or allow access outside the EEA. Before publication, confirm every active provider's processing location, transfer and applicable safeguard, such as an adequacy decision or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 9. Rights and complaints

Subject to applicable law, visitors may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability; consent may be withdrawn at any time. Requests go to [ezzat@exceptionalgrp.com](mailto:ezzat@exceptionalgrp.com), and identity verification may be needed. Visitors may complain to their relevant supervisory authority; because the controller is established in Poland, they may also contact the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

## 10. Security and changes

We use measures appropriate to the processing, including server-side enquiry handling and validation and anti-abuse controls. No online transmission or storage is completely secure. Updates will state the effective date.
